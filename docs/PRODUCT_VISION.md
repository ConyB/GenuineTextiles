## Problems today

1. Documents are written by hand on pre-printed pads, and some are typed on a letterhead.
2. Debts are kept in a book and in memory (about 13 customers owe money at any time).
3. There is no stock book. Fabric and materials are not tracked, and there is no accounting software.
4. Orders, production and deliveries are followed through daily workshop records.
5. There are no financial statements, and no accountant yet.

## What the system must do

Give the owner and his team one place for: quotations, proformas, invoices, receipts, delivery notes, stock and goods received, orders and production, debtors, expenses, sales reports and monthly financial statements, plus messaging and marketing tools.

## Success measures (for the owner to approve)

- Every printed document matches his current look and numbering and can be issued in under two minutes.
- He can see who owes him, how much, and for how long, at any moment.
- Fabric and material stock is known, and low-stock warnings fire at 25%.
- Staff see only what their role allows, and cost and profit stay hidden from them.
- Monthly statements can be produced without retyping data.

## Who will use it

Owner, General Manager, Store Keeper, Sales staff, Workshop Supervisor, Accountant (not yet hired), Marketing Manager. Four people will be trained first. Salespeople and the store keeper need the most training.

## Constraints

English only. A4 printing. Reliable internet, with generator backup. Used on desktops, laptops, tablets and phones. Target go-live: **01/01/2027**.
